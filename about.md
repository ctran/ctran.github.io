I'm Cuong Tran, a software engineer at ServiceNow based in San Diego, CA.

## What I work on

- **Open source.** I maintain [annotate_models](https://github.com/ctran/annotate_models), which annotates Rails classes with schema and routes info, and [TinyRadius](https://github.com/ctran/TinyRadius), a small Java Radius library.
- **Developer tooling.** I package prebuilt binaries for Nix in [nixpkgs-bin](https://github.com/ctran/nixpkgs-bin) and keep my setup in [dotfiles](https://github.com/ctran/dotfiles).
- **ServiceNow SDK.** Sample apps for the [Now SDK](https://github.com/ctran/now-sdk-sample-app) and the [Fluent SDK](https://github.com/ctran/now-fluentsdk-sample-app).

## Contact

Find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/ctran). Issues and pull requests are the best way to reach me about any project.
